🥬 Food Access Atlas

3,136 U.S. counties — food deserts, low-access populations, and supermarket distance. USDA data.

Data from the USDA Economic Research Service Food Access Research Atlas (2019). A "food desert" is a low-income census tract where residents have low access to a supermarket. This is an independent tool — not affiliated with any government agency.
